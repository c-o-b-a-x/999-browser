A quick tour

Here is where to find the browser’s everyday tools. You can change these choices later in Settings.

Privacy firstBrowsing data is kept on this device. Tracker blocking and privacy signals are enabled by default; they reduce tracking but do not make you anonymous.

Tabs and navigation

The row at the top holds your tabs. Use Ctrl + T to open one and Ctrl + W to close it.

Move aroundUse the back, forward, and reload buttons beside the address bar. Press Ctrl + L to focus it.

Search, history, and bookmarks

Search from the homepage or type a search or website address into the toolbar. Searches go to DuckDuckGo.

Your libraryUse the library button near Settings to find bookmarks and recent history. Save a page with the star or press Ctrl + D.

Settings and privacy

The gear button opens the main Settings area. Its sections organize General, Appearance, Privacy & security, and Passwords.

Make it yoursAppearance includes themes and homepage controls. Privacy & security includes ad blocking, data clearing, and local diagnostic logs.

Set up your password manager

Choose a strong master password (at least 12 characters) and a 4–6 digit recovery PIN. The PIN helps recover access if you forget the master password. Keep both somewhere safe.

Passwords are encrypted on this device. The master password protects the Passwords manager; confirmed autofill remains available through OS secure storage while the manager is locked.

Password manager already set up You can manage saved passwords any time in Settings → Passwords.
